## Platform

web

## Users

Community visitors, center administrators, and the platform superadmin.
The LM tracker serves people recording their daily practices without signing in.

## Product Purpose

Support masjids and centers worldwide with public information and center management.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve public access to center information and role-controlled administration.
- Use services that do not require paid plans.
- `/lmtracker` stores personal daily checklists in this browser only, without login or cross-device synchronization.
- The tracker shows seven dates with today in the middle, and independent daily completion.
- Preserve all 44 supplied checklist entries; the supplied total of 43 conflicts with the item count.

## Brand Commitments

Premium, simple white-and-blue styling with dark mode.
